Weather

If you use the weather feature, the App requests a public forecast from api.open-meteo.com using the coordinates of the district you selected (from a fixed list bundled in the App). The App does not access your device GPS or precise location.

News

If you use the news feature, the App fetches public RSS news feeds through the third-party proxy rss2json.com, which forwards requests to Indian news sites. The proxy operator can see the request (including your IP address) and the feed URL.

External links
