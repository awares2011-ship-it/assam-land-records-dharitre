## Full description (4000 max)

Assam Land Records – Dharitre is the fastest way to look up your land records in Assam, right from your phone. Pick district → revenue circle → village, enter your dag or patta number, solve the official CAPTCHA, and the Jamabandi appears natively inside the app — no browser tab, no paperwork queue, no trip to the circle office.

Everything comes live from the Government of Assam Dharitree (ILRMS) portal. Nothing is scraped, nothing is faked, no CAPTCHA is bypassed. You hold the record exactly as the circle office shows it.

★ HOW IT WORKS
1) Pick district, revenue circle and village. 2) Enter your dag or patta number and solve the CAPTCHA shown from the portal. 3) Read your record natively, then save it as PNG or PDF — in seconds.
★ KEY FEATURES
• Search Jamabandi (Record of Rights) by dag number or patta number
• Browse 30+ districts, every revenue circle, every village — fetched live from the official portal
• Download any record as a PNG image or PDF, or share it straight to WhatsApp
• Offline Assam-standard land calculator — bigha, katha, lecha, acre, hectare, sq ft, sq m
• Plot-area calculator — length × breadth converts into every unit instantly
• Mission Basundhara, Bhunaksha, Mutation (Namjari) and e-Khajana guides with one-tap links to the official portals
• Government schemes directory — PM-KISAN, Orunodoi, PMAY-G, IGNOAPS, PM-JAY, Fasal Bima, MGNREGA, scholarships and more, each with eligibility, benefits, required documents, step-by-step apply instructions and verified links
• 7-day weather forecast for every Assam district
• Latest news from NE Now, The Hindu, Indian Express, NDTV and Hindustan Times
• Stamp duty estimator and EMI calculator
• Assamese glossary, searchable FAQ, saved services and quick feedback

★ SMART, COLOUR-CODED NOTIFICATIONS
Opt in once and receive timely alerts on your lock screen: fresh Assam and India news headlines, a government scheme you might qualify for, a short daily weather forecast for your district, and handy in-app tool reminders.

★ THREE LANGUAGES
English · অসমীয়া · हिन्दी — switch anytime from the top bar. Pages, glossary and FAQ are localised in all three.

★ PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No sign-up. No analytics beyond ad serving. Every land-record request goes directly from your phone to the official Dharitree portal; nothing is stored on any server. Downloads are saved locally to your Documents folder. The app is free and supported by Google AdMob ads — you can opt out of ad personalisation anytime in Google Settings → Ads.

★ BUILT FOR EVERYONE
Perfect for farmers, landowners, patwaris, lawyers, students and anyone who needs quick, trustworthy Assam land information. Works on slow networks and lightweight phones, and the calculator, glossary and scheme guides work fully offline.

★ IMPORTANT NOTICE
This is an unofficial citizen companion app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of Assam. For legally binding documents, visit the official Dharitree portal at ilrms.assam.gov.in
